# Changelog

## 1.1.0 – November 25, 2026

* added: A linked mockup can show a label and an icon over its screen when hovered, with style controls for the background, colors and label typography.
* added: Themes and other plugins can render a mockup from PHP with arts_device_mockups_render() or get its markup with arts_device_mockups_get().

## 1.0.3 – November 24, 2026

* improved: confirmed compatibility with WordPress 7.2.
* fixed: Existing pages show the latest mockup look right after a plugin update, without clearing Elementor's cache by hand.

## 1.0.2 – November 8, 2026

* fixed: Mockups re-rendered by Elementor keep working when another plugin or an optimizer changes the script loading order.

## 1.0.1 – October 13, 2026

* fixed: A mockup no longer causes a script error when its element is missing while the page loads or is edited.

## 1.0.0 – August 21, 2026

Initial release.
